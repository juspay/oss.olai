What the control means — the subject, then the filter

The wake is on kolu terminal activity — that is the subject, and it is the kolu plugin's own control, speaking its own words. The .olai file is the FILTER: which terminals, namely the kind-kolu-terminal values on that file's un-done nodes, mirrors resolving to their targets — which makes the day board (lanes.olai) the natural filter: it already holds exactly the live lanes. No file selected = no doorbell. A second conversation may filter by a different file and become its own seat.
